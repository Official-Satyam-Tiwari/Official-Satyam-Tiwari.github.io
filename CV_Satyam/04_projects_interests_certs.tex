\noindent \resheading{Research Interests}
\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{CT-less PET Imaging:} Quantitative attenuation correction using detector-scattered and annihilation data in plastic-scintillator PET systems.

\item \textbf{Image Reconstruction:} Simulation-driven reconstruction methods across the J-PET scanner family and prototypes for improving lesion localization, attenuation-map estimation, and overall PET image quality.

\item \textbf{AI for Medical Imaging:} Deep-learning-based enhancement and denoising of attenuation maps, with a focus on robust CT-less workflows for molecular imaging.

\end{itemize}
